Symptoms of Depression

  • Feeling Sad

    Experiencing prolonged sadness or emotional pain without a clear cause.

  • Loss of Interest

    Reduced interest in activities or hobbies you once enjoyed.

  • Appetite Changes

    Unexplained changes in eating habits or weight fluctuations.

  • Difficulty Concentrating

    Struggling to focus or make decisions in daily life.

  • Persistent Fatigue

    Feeling physically or mentally drained most of the time.
